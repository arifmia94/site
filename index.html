Profile

About

I'm Md Arif Mia, a graphic design and documentation professional with over eight years of combined experience across print, publishing, and NGO communications.

I built my foundation in prepress and print production at Asia Printing Press, then led client-facing print collections as Senior Graphic Designer at College Gate Binding & Printing. Today I lead design and documentation for Sangram NGO's flagship rural development programme, after several years managing its media and social communications output.
